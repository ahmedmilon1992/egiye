Programme Terms — Pilot 1

Version 2026-09-01, shown in the app before you apply.

1. Egiye is a free, invitation-based programme for adults (18+) living in Bangladesh. Applying does not guarantee a place; the Egiye team approves a limited number of participants.

2. Only walks you start in the app with Start walk count. Walks are verified automatically and, where needed, by a person. Walks that are not at a walking pace, have no usable GPS, do not show enough movement for the steps counted, overlap another walk, or are uploaded more than 24 hours after they ended may be rejected or reduced.

3. Approved walks earn 4 Egiye Coins per 100 approved steps, up to 5,000 steps (200 coins) per Bangladesh calendar day, and up to 6,000 coins per participant for the pilot. 10 coins = ৳1.

4. Coins are paid in taka to your bKash personal number by the Egiye team once your balance reaches 200 coins, normally in a weekly run; the whole remaining balance is paid at the end of the pilot. Coins cannot be transferred and have no value outside this programme. Unpaid coins expire 60 days after the pilot ends.

5. One account per person. Using another person's phone, simulating steps (for example shaking the phone), or otherwise manipulating the app may lead to rejected walks, suspension and forfeiture of unpaid coins.

6. Egiye may pause or end the pilot; coins already earned stay payable under clause 4.

7. Your data is handled as described in the Privacy Policy.
