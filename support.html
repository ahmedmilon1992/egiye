Common questions

I forgot my password. Email us from the address you signed up with. We will set a temporary password and tell you; the app asks you to choose a new one at the next login. We never ask for your bKash PIN or any OTP code.

My walk says "under review" or "rejected". Walks are checked automatically (walking pace, GPS, movement, overlap with other walks). If something looks unusual a person reviews it. You can tap Appeal on the walk and tell us what happened.

Can I use Egiye without being in the paid pilot? Yes. Anyone can create an account, record walks and see each walk verified. Only participants approved for a funded pilot receive cash rewards.

When do approved participants get paid? For approved participants, when the balance reaches 200 coins (৳20) the Egiye team sends the money to your bKash number, usually once a week. The payment and its bKash TrxID appear under Balance → Transaction history and Rewards → Payments received.

Wrong bKash number? Update it in the app (Rewards → Payout method) before the next payment run, or email us.

Delete my account

1. In the Egiye app open Profile and tap Delete account, then confirm. Or email ahmedmilon1992@gmail.com from the address you signed up with and ask us to delete your account.

2. Your account is locked at once. Our team pays any outstanding reward to your bKash number (or you may forfeit it) and completes the deletion within 30 days.

3. Deleted: your name, email, address, date of birth and walk evidence (GPS points and step detail). Kept in anonymised form for accounting: step totals, coin ledger and bKash payment records (amount, TrxID, date), for as long as required by law.
