Walk outdoors, earn rewards.

Egiye is a small, invitation-based walking programme in Bangladesh. Tap Start walk, walk outside, and every approved walk earns Egiye Coins (10 coins = ৳1) that are paid to your bKash number.

How it works

1. Apply in the app. Places are limited and approved by the Egiye team.
2. Tap Start walk before you walk. Only steps recorded during a started walk count; the walk ends itself after 3 minutes without steps.
3. Each approved walk earns 4 coins per 100 steps, up to 5,000 steps (200 coins) a day.
4. Once your balance reaches 200 coins (৳20) the Egiye team sends the money to your bKash number — you don't need to request anything. Every payment appears in the app with its bKash TrxID.

Pilot 1 (October 2026)

20 funded places · 30 days · ৳12,000 total reward fund · Dhaka time zone. Rules are shown in the app before you apply and never change during the pilot.
