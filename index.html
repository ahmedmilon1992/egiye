Verified outdoor walking for Bangladesh.

Egiye (এগিয়ে, "move forward") is a walking program for adults in Bangladesh. Tap Start walk, walk outside, and every walk is checked — you see your GPS route, whether the distance matches your steps and your walking pace.

How it works

1. Create an account in the app and start walking right away.
2. Tap Start walk before you walk. Only a walk you start is recorded; it ends by itself after 3 minutes without steps. Your location is never tracked at any other time.
3. After every walk you see the walk proof: your route on a map and each verification check, passed or failed. If you think a decision is wrong, you can appeal it.

Pilot 1 (October 2026)

Egiye runs as a limited pilot by PrimeCell Distributor LLC. Participants approved by the Egiye team also receive cash rewards for verified walks: 4 coins per 100 verified steps, up to 200 coins a day (10 coins = ৳1), sent by our team to their own bKash number.
