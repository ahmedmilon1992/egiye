Privacy Policy

Effective 11 October 2026 · Applies to the Egiye apps for iPhone and Android and the Egiye service.

Who we are

Egiye ("we") is operated by PrimeCell Distributor LLC. Contact: ahmedmilon1992@gmail.com.

What we collect and why

Account: email address and password (stored as a one-way hash), full name, phone number, date of birth (to confirm you are 18+), address/district and the reason you joined. Used to run your account and, if you apply for a funded pilot, to review your application and pay you.

Payout details: your bKash personal number (your phone number unless you give another). Used only to send your rewards; shown masked in the app.

Walk data (only during a walk you start): step counts and distance from your phone's motion sensors, and your GPS location, speed and accuracy. Used to verify that the walk happened outdoors at a walking pace and to calculate your coins. Recording continues while the screen is locked (on Android with a visible notification) and stops when you end the walk or after 3 minutes without steps. We do not track your location at any other time and we do not read Apple Health or Google Health Connect.

Device information: an app install identifier and the phone's operating system version, to detect duplicate accounts and fix problems.

Coins and payments: an immutable record of every coin earned, every payment sent (amount, bKash TrxID, date) and every staff decision, so rewards are auditable.

What we do not do

We do not sell or rent your data. We do not show advertising and do not use an advertising ID. We do not share your data with sponsors. We never ask for your bKash PIN or any OTP code.

Who can see your data

Egiye staff with a role (support, operations, finance, owner) see what they need to approve applications, review walks and pay you; every staff action is logged. Raw GPS points are visible only to staff reviewing a walk. Your data is stored with our hosting provider Supabase (database and authentication) in the Singapore region, protected by encryption in transit and at rest.

How long we keep it

Account and walk data: while your account exists and up to 60 days after the pilot ends. Payment and ledger records: as long as required for accounting and tax (currently 7 years), in anonymised form after you delete your account.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account in the app (Profile → Delete account) or by emailing ahmedmilon1992@gmail.com from the address you signed up with. The request locks your account immediately and our team completes the deletion within 30 days, after any pending reward has been paid to you or forfeited at your request. Deletion removes your name, email, address and walk evidence (GPS and step detail); step totals and payment records are kept in anonymised form for accounting.

Your choices

You can edit your profile and bKash number in the app and decline location or motion permission (walks then cannot be verified). You may also ask us for a copy of your data.

Children

Egiye is for adults 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18.

Changes

If this policy changes we will update the date above and show the change in the app before it takes effect.
